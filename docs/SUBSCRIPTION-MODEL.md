# Axiom: enterprise subscription

Axiom is available under an annual **enterprise subscription** for production use. One subscription covers product access, updates and upgrades, and the stated support level. There is no separate support surcharge.

## License

Axiom is source-available under the [Zyvor Production License v1.0](../LICENSE). Evaluation, development, testing, research and other non-production use are free. Production use needs a commercial license from Zyvor AI Labs Private Limited, which is what the subscription below provides.

## Plans

| Plan | What you get | Coverage |
|------|--------------|----------|
| Community | Evaluation and non-production use, where the licence allows it | Community resources |
| Professional | Entry edition, updates and upgrades | Business hours, next-business-day critical response |
| Enterprise | Advanced edition, updates and upgrades | Priority business hours, four-business-hour critical response |
| Premium | Contracted enterprise scope and critical escalation | 24x7 critical intake, one-hour initial response target |

Priced per node per year: **INR 19,999 per node including 18% GST** (about USD 202, GST-free). Team and volume price: INR 14,999 per node (about USD 151) with a minimum of 10 nodes (INR 149,990), by invoice. Axiom is separately licensed and is not part of the Zyvor Support meter. Non-production use stays free. Quote or invoice: [sales@zyvor.dev](mailto:sales@zyvor.dev).

## Terms

- Annual prepaid subscription is the default, with a 12-month minimum. Multi-year prepay is available.
- Minimums apply after any discounts.
- Implementation, migration, training and managed operations are separate services.
- At expiry, updates, downloads and support end. Open-source rights, where they apply, continue under the licence.
- Response targets need a contracted scope and are not uptime guarantees.
- Existing signed agreements keep their agreed terms.
